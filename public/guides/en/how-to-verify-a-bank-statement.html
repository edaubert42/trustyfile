How to verify a bank statement

Updated 11 November 2026

An edited bank statement inflates income, hides an overdraft or proves a payment that never happened. It is easy to imitate visually and hard to forge cleanly: it is made of figures that all have to add up against each other.

In short

  • Redo the statement's arithmetic: the opening balance, plus credits, minus debits, must give the closing balance exactly.
  • A statement downloaded from online banking is produced by the bank's own system; a PDF made in Word, Canva or an online converter contradicts what it claims to be.
  • In a French IBAN, the bank code (the 5 digits after the check digits) identifies one specific bank: it must match the bank named on the statement.
  • In France, a landlord may not ask a prospective tenant or their guarantor for bank statements (law of 6 July 1989, article 22-2).

Who asks for bank statements?

Bank statements mostly change hands in loan applications, at mortgage brokers, between businesses checking a customer's ability to pay, and in private sales where the buyer "proves" a transfer.

Renting in France is different: bank statements and certificates of good account standing are among the documents a landlord may not require from an applicant or their guarantor (law of 6 July 1989, article 22-2, and decree no. 2015-1437). To check a tenant's income, use the pay slips and the tax notice.

Redo the arithmetic

A statement is a chain of additions. Whoever changes one amount usually forgets to fix everything that depends on it:

  • Opening balance + credits − debits = closing balance: the basic rule, to the cent.
  • Column totals: the debit and credit totals at the bottom of the page must equal the sum of the lines.
  • From one statement to the next: one month's closing balance is the next month's opening balance. Asking for two or three consecutive statements makes an edit much harder.
  • The running balance, when the bank prints one: a single edited line shifts every line after it.

Identify the software that made the PDF

A statement downloaded from online banking comes out of the bank's document system: same PDF producer, same fonts, same structure month after month. Metadata naming a word processor, a PDF editor or an online converter contradicts that origin.

Be wary of statement templates sold online too: they copy a bank's layout, not its technical fingerprint. The tells are covered in our guide to modified PDFs.

Look at the sensitive areas

Most fraud does not build a whole statement: it edits a genuine one. The tells cluster around the account holder's name, the address, the salary line and the balances: a different font in the middle of a column, misaligned figures, a white box laid over the old value, a revision added to the file after it was created.

Cross-check against the other documents

  • The IBAN: its check digits must be valid, and the bank code must match the bank whose logo is on the statement (check an IBAN).
  • The salary credit: its amount must match the net pay on the same month's pay slip, and its label the employer named there.
  • The account holder and address: the same as on the rest of the file.

Scans and screenshots: unverifiable

A scanned, photographed or screenshotted statement keeps no metadata, no fonts and no revision history. Only the balance arithmetic can still be checked, and finding nothing wrong then proves nothing.

When the stakes are real, ask for the PDF downloaded from online banking, or use a licensed account-access service (PSD2), where the data comes straight from the bank with no document in between to forge.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if a bank statement is fake?
Redo the balance arithmetic, check which software produced the PDF, validate the IBAN and its bank code, then look for edits around the name, the salary line and the balances.
Can a landlord in France ask for bank statements?
No. Bank statements and certificates of good account standing are among the documents a landlord may not require from a prospective tenant or their guarantor.
Can a screenshot of a statement or transfer be verified?
Barely: an image keeps no metadata and no fonts. Only the consistency of the figures can be checked. Ask for the PDF from online banking.
