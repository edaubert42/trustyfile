How to verify a pay slip

Updated 11 November 2026

The forged pay slip has become a staple of rental files and loan applications: an inflated salary on one's own slip, or a swapped name on somebody else's. Like every edit it leaves traces — and the French pay slip has one feature that makes it more verifiable than most documents.

In short

  • On a French pay slip, look for the 2D-Doc code first: if the amount it encodes differs from the printed amount, the slip has been edited.
  • Payroll software (Silae, PayFit, ADP, Sage, Cegid) leaves a stable fingerprint; a pay slip produced by a word processor or an online converter contradicts what it claims to be.
  • Redo the arithmetic: gross minus contributions, taxable net, year-to-date totals. An inflated salary that was not recalculated breaks the sums.
  • Check the employer's SIRET in the public company directory, and ask for the original PDF rather than a scan.

Look for the 2D-Doc code first

Many French payroll systems print a 2D-Doc code on the slip: a small DataMatrix square, usually near the top or bottom of the page. It holds the key data — name, period, net amount — cryptographically signed by the issuer.

Its presence changes everything: if the salary printed on the page does not match the one encoded in the square, somebody edited the visual without being able to touch the code. It is one of the few checks close to proof. The details are in our 2D-Doc guide.

Its absence, on the other hand, proves nothing: not every employer uses software that generates one.

Identify the software that produced the slip

Pay slips come out of industrial payroll pipelines — Silae, PayFit, ADP, Sage, Cegid — whose technical fingerprint is very stable: same PDF producer, same embedded fonts, same structure month after month.

A slip whose metadata announces a word processor, an online converter or a screenshot tool contradicts what it claims to be. Employers do not type pay slips in Word.

Redo the arithmetic

A pay slip is a public addition. A forger who inflates the net pay almost always forgets to fix everything that depends on it:

  • Gross minus contributions = net: each contribution line applies a rate to a base; a net edited without recomputing the lines breaks the sum.
  • The taxable and social net amounts must stay consistent with the net pay.
  • Year-to-date totals: a cumulative figure lower than the month's amount, or one that does not grow from one slip to the next, betrays an isolated edit.
  • Salary versus job title: an amount out of proportion with the position and grade deserves a question.

Examine the name and amount areas

As with proof of address, the common fraud does not build a slip from scratch: it swaps a name or an amount on a genuine one. The evidence therefore concentrates on those areas: a font change mid-line, a white rectangle covering the old value, a pasted image block whose resolution does not match the rest of the page.

Verify the employer

A French pay slip must state the employer's company registration number (SIRET), activity code and collective agreement. All of it can be checked from outside:

  • Does the SIRET exist in the public registry, and does it match the displayed name?
  • Is the activity code plausible for the job held?
  • Is the collective agreement the one for that sector?
  • Was the company still active during the slip's period?

Scans, once again: unverifiable

A scanned or photographed slip keeps no text layer, no fonts, no payroll metadata. Most of the checks above become inapplicable, and the absence of anomalies then means nothing. When you are in a position to insist, ask for the original PDF — the one the employee downloads from the HR portal — rather than a flattened copy.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if a pay slip is fake?
Check the 2D-Doc code if there is one, the software that produced the PDF, the arithmetic and year-to-date totals, and that the employer exists, through its SIRET. Edits concentrate on the name and the net pay.
Do all French pay slips carry a 2D-Doc code?
No. Not every payroll package generates one: its absence proves nothing, its presence allows a check that comes close to proof.
Can a scanned pay slip be verified?
Barely: a scan loses the text layer, the fonts and the metadata. Ask for the PDF the employee downloads from their HR portal.
