How to spot a fake rental application

Updated 11 November 2026

A fake rental application almost never shows at a glance: it is made of genuine documents, edited just enough to clear the income threshold. The reliable method is to check each document with its own checks, then cross-check the documents against each other.

In short

  • A fake French rental application usually rests on edited pay slips, an edited tax notice or an edited proof of address: each document has its own checks.
  • The tax notice can be checked on the tax office's official SVAIR service, with the tax number and the notice reference: it is the most reliable check in the file.
  • Cross-check the documents: the same name and address everywhere, a salary consistent with the declared income, an employer that exists in the business register.
  • A landlord in France may only ask for the documents listed in decree no. 2015-1437: bank statements and certificates of good account standing are excluded.

What a landlord may ask for

The list is closed: decree no. 2015-1437 sets what a landlord may require from an applicant and their guarantor. It includes an ID document, a proof of address, proof of occupation (employment contract, employer certificate, Kbis extract for the self-employed) and proof of income (latest pay slips, latest tax notice).

Anything not on the list is forbidden, including bank statements, certificates of good account standing, the health insurance card, a criminal record extract, medical records or a holding deposit cheque.

Check each document

  • Pay slips: 2D-Doc code, payroll software, arithmetic and year-to-date totals (our guide).
  • Tax notice: SVAIR check and 2D-Doc code (our guide).
  • Proof of address: an energy bill or rent receipt edited to the applicant's name (our guide).
  • ID document: the MRZ and its consistency with the other documents (our guide).
  • Self-employed: a recent Kbis extract and an active SIRET (our guide).

Cross-check the documents

One forged document can be careful; a whole consistent file is much harder to fabricate. The most common contradictions:

  • Name and address differ from one document to the next, or the proof of address does not match the address on the tax notice.
  • The salary on the pay slips, over a year, bears no relation to the income on the tax notice, allowing for the one-year lag.
  • The employer: its SIRET does not exist, belongs to another company, or the company has closed (check a SIRET).
  • The dates: seniority on the pay slips that contradicts the hiring date in the contract, or three months of pay slips identical except for the period.

Warning signs

  • Documents sent only as photos or scans when they exist as PDFs: a flattened copy erases most traces of editing.
  • PDFs made by an online converter or a PDF editor, where payroll software or the tax website is expected.
  • Income just above the threshold asked for, often three times the rent.
  • An employer phone number supplied in the file: call the switchboard you found yourself instead.

DossierFacile: useful, not proof

DossierFacile is the French state's free service for putting a rental application together. Its staff check that documents are legible, consistent and on the authorised list, and files are shared with a watermark. It does not certify that the documents are authentic, so the checks above still matter.

What a forger risks

Making or altering a document and using it is forgery under French law (article 441-1 of the criminal code), punishable by three years in prison and a 45,000 euro fine. Keep the documents and messages you received if you plan to file a complaint.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if a rental application is fake?
Check each document with its own checks (SVAIR for the tax notice, 2D-Doc and arithmetic for pay slips, the MRZ for the ID), then cross-check names, addresses, income and employer across the documents.
Which documents may a landlord in France not ask for?
Anything not listed in decree no. 2015-1437, including bank statements, certificates of good account standing, the health insurance card, a criminal record extract, medical records or a holding deposit cheque.
Does DossierFacile guarantee that an application is genuine?
No. DossierFacile checks that documents are legible, consistent and on the authorised list, but does not certify that they are authentic. Checking the tax notice on SVAIR is still worth it.
